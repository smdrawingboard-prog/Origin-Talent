Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to the questions Johannesburg families ask us most.

How long does it take to place a nanny or domestic staff member?
Most Origin Talent placements are made within 24 to 72 hours of a completed brief, thanks to our pre-screened candidate pool.
How are Origin Talent candidates screened?
Every candidate undergoes reference checks, criminal background screening, ID verification and an in-person or video interview before being presented to a family.
Which areas of Johannesburg does Origin Talent service?
We place household staff with families across Johannesburg, including Dainfern, Fourways, Sandton, Bryanston and surrounding areas.
What does it cost to hire staff through Origin Talent?
Placement fees depend on the role and seniority required. WhatsApp or call us for a free, no-obligation quote tailored to your household.
